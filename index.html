How it calculates

  • Fit: weights = total params × bytes/param. Usable HBM = GPUs × memory × usable fraction. What's left holds KV cache: (prompt + output) × KV bytes/token per user. That sets the user limit.
  • Decode step = max(memory time, compute time) + fixed per-step overhead. Memory time = (weights touched + KV read) ÷ (aggregate bandwidth × efficiency). For MoE models, the share of experts touched grows with batch size as 1 − (1 − active/total)batch, so at large batches the whole model is read every step. Compute = (2 × active params + attention FLOPs) × batch ÷ (FP8 FLOPS × efficiency).
  • Per-user tok/s = 1 ÷ step time. Total tok/s = that × users. Bigger memory doesn't raise per-user speed, but it allows more users, and that raises total throughput.
  • TTFT = prefill FLOPs ÷ (FP8 FLOPS × prefill efficiency), plus one full read of the weights, on an otherwise idle rig. Under load it will be longer.
  • Calibration: default efficiencies were tuned so the model reproduces two published DeepSeek-R1 points on 8× B200: ~250 tok/s for a single user (with MTP) and ~36k tok/s total at 3,072 concurrent users. Other models and GPUs are extrapolations. Treat results as ±30–50% planning numbers, not benchmarks.
Shared assumptions (editable)

Auto overhead: 1.5 ms on one GPU; 3 + 0.25 ms per GPU over NVLink; 3 + 1.5 ms per extra GPU over PCIe.

Where the preset numbers come from

GPUs: NVIDIA datasheets (B300 288 GB at 8 TB/s, 4.5 PF dense FP8; B200 HGX 180 GB; H200 141 GB at 4.8 TB/s; H100 80 GB at 3.35 TB/s; RTX PRO 6000 96 GB at 1.79 TB/s). The RTX PRO 6000 FP8 figure is derived from its 4,000 sparse FP4 TOPS. Models: official model cards for total/active params and context (Kimi K3 and K3.1 Pro 2.8T/104B, 24 MLA/DSA attention layers of 93; Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B, 23 full-attention layers with 4 KV heads; GLM-5.2 744B/40B with 1M context; DeepSeek V4 Pro 1.6T/49B, V4 Flash 284B/13B, GLM-5 744B/40B, Kimi K2.6 1T/32B, Qwen3.5-397B-A17B, Gemma 4 26B-A4B 25.2B/3.8B, DeepSeek-R1 671B/37B). KV bytes/token are computed from each architecture's attention config. The DeepSeek V4 KV and attention values are estimates (DeepSeek reports about 90% less KV than V3.2). These are marked "est." in the model list. "Native FP4/FP8 mix" assumes ≈0.6 bytes/param.
